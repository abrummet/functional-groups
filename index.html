How to play

1Tap the atoms that make up one functional group, or drag across them. Drag on empty space to draw a lasso.
2Tap the group's name, or drag the name onto the molecule.
3Find every group to finish the molecule. Streaks multiply your points.

Which atoms to select

Lines are carbon–carbon bonds; each corner or line end is a carbon. Hydrogens on carbon aren't drawn, except the H of an aldehyde. For alcohols, ethers, amines and amides, you may also include the carbon attached to the O or N, as long as that carbon isn't part of another group (such as a benzene ring).
